Windstar, HOME!
Dave's

 

Oh, there was much rejoicing when my friends and I found out that we finally had another choice for seeing Formula 1 racing live.  No longer would we be held financially hostage by opportunistic event organizers, restaurant managers, and hotel operators in Montreal.  This year we were going to be taken advantage of by Americans.  Specifically, Indianapolis Americans.

You see, they had decided to bring Formula 1 back to the States, back to the largest market of consumers in the world.  And we wanted to be part of it.  They had taken their legendary Indianapolis Speedway and carved a road track in and around their beloved oval.

Can't really see the road track from there.  Check out this map.

Looked good, in theory.  Tight turns, a back straightaway, a huge banked turn leading into an even longer front straightaway.  The ticket prices were more than reasonable, we scrambled to reserve a hotel room, and for a chance to see stuff like this

we were more than willing to spend some cash.  With all arrangements made, we rented a surprisingly cramped Ford Windstar from the corporate whores at Hertz and myself and four friends were off to the races!
