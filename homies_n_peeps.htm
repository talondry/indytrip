The annual Halloween bash moved to Fonthill this year.  Many beers and good times were had.  Check out the Fonthill Freak Fest.

Every once and awhile my friends and I will actually scrape enough cash and ambition together to go see a formula 1 race.  Up until last year our choices for a live event were limited to the Montreal Grand Prix.  This year, however, we decided to check out the return of Formula 1 racing to the United States by going to Indianapolis.  Check out the slides in the Indy Trip 2000 section.

My friend Schmeck (not his real name, but close) recently recounted this life-altering experience that was his annual physical examination.  Word is that Schmeck is recovering slowly but has stabilized emotionally with the support of family and friends.  In his words, this is Schmeck's Tale!  (snicker, snicker).

In my first year of university, I lived in residence with 29 other drunken students.  And for that entire year we kept a quote board to record all the stupid things we said.  In second year, our numbers dwindled to 15 as the engineers moved off campus to more humble surroundings.  But the beer consumption doubled, so the output to our quote board was pretty much the same as first year.  In third year, the quote board stuck with it's most prolific contributor, Acad, and was kept in two different households.  In fourth year, the quote board was a mere shadow of it's formal self as Acad was no longer a regular contributor.  And then, for five long years, there was no mention of the quote board... until now.  Even though we are miles apart, I think we can still contribute to an electronic quote board and to start it off, the greatest quote board contributor ever, Acad.
